WHO ARE WE

Strategy meets
execution. Results follow.

McGeorge Consulting Ltd is an ISO-certified multidisciplinary consulting and technology firm established in 2005. We specialize in Engineering, Procurement, Construction, ICT, Clean & Renewable Energy, Agribusiness, and Human Capacity Development.

With offices across Nigeria, we serve public and private sector organizations seeking operational excellence and digital transformation. Our core strength lies in combining strategic advisory with execution — delivering innovative, scalable solutions that drive measurable growth.

  • Engineering & Infrastructure

  • ERP & Digital Transformation
